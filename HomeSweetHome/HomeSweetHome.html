Home Sweet Home

About

Context

This game is the result of the end project from the third semester

Task

We had 6 weeks to make a playable prototype, starting from the general game idea, to the first blockout, all the way to the finished prototype.

Gameplay

The game consists of two game loops:
The cozy loop, all about completing mini games to earn points and buy new furniture to decorate your home.
The action loop, where the player must reach reach the deepest layer of the world, while fending off hordes of enemies using furniture.
